## Origin and independence

The ATTENSION faceplate SVG and PNG were independently drawn for ATTENSION,
using a scan of the Bastl Instruments Kastle 2 faceplate only to establish
physical control placement and general visual reference. Some LED artwork was
approximated from the Bastl faceplate and redrawn for ATTENSION.

The fox logo was generated specifically for ATTENSION with AI assistance under
Chris Beattie's creative direction. The manual text, layout, and original
illustrations were developed specifically for ATTENSION through collaborative
revisions.

ATTENSION is an independent project and is not endorsed by Bastl Instruments.
Nothing here claims exclusive ownership of Bastl hardware design, trademarks,
or third-party artwork.
